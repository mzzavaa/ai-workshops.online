The Method

3 workshops. 3 weeks.
One working prototype.

A structured flow that moves your organization from "we want AI" to a decision-ready prototype. No months of back-and-forth.

01 2 hours

Goal Workshop

Map your AI ambitions to concrete business problems. Prioritize use cases by value and feasibility. Leave with a clear focus.
02 3 hours

Concept Workshop

Design the architecture, select AWS services, estimate costs, and define exactly what the prototype will look like.
03 2 hours

Decision Workshop

Demo a working prototype live. Make go/no-go decisions per use case. Walk away with a concrete implementation roadmap.
3 workshops total
~7h with your team
3 weeks from first call to prototype
1 working prototype, ready to demo
