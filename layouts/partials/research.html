What the research shows about AI delivery

The failure patterns are consistent across every major research organization. Gartner reports that approximately half of all GenAI proofs of concept are abandoned before reaching production. BCG's 2024 global survey of more than 1,000 executives found that success depends 70% on people and processes, 20% on technology, and only 10% on the algorithms themselves. IBM's 2025 CEO Study, conducted across 2,000 CEOs in 33 countries, found that only 16% of AI initiatives have scaled enterprise-wide. McKinsey's 2025 State of AI survey confirms that nearly two-thirds of organizations have not yet begun scaling AI across their enterprise.

The common failure mode is not the technology. It is the absence of a structured delivery path: defined outputs, clear decision checkpoints, and a framework that moves at business speed. The Open Practice Library, Red Hat's community-sourced framework for structured delivery, identifies outcome-based planning and iterative experimentation as the critical differentiators between AI initiatives that deliver value and those that stall permanently in the pilot phase.
